Goal · example
  • Rebrand Paws & Co, a dog-walking shop.
  • Must look friendly on a van and on Instagram.
  • Used on: van, website and social avatars.
    Not needed: packaging (later).
  • Tone: warm, local, a bit playful.
  • What it must do
  • Where it will be used
